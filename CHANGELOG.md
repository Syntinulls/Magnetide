# Magnetide — Changelog

## Version 0.3.1

### New Features

#### Enemies

- Added the Floater, a slow and very tough enemy that appears once the first storm is behind
  you. It drifts to the ship and bursts against the hull, hurting anyone close and scattering
  pines, so shoot it down before it arrives.

### Fixes

#### Weapons

- The Shotgun fires its pellets again. It flashed, roared and burned through its
  magazine while spawning nothing at all, so it hit like an empty gun; every other
  weapon was unaffected, because the Shotgun is the only one that fires a spread
  rather than a single bullet.
- Grenade Launcher explosions now burn from yellow through orange to grey smoke instead of
  flashing plain white.
